Capabilities

End-to-End Structural Planning & BIM Modeling

From early schematic framing comparisons to fabrication-ready reinforcement and structural steel packages.

01 / COMPUTATIONAL FEA
Computational Structural Modeling

Full 3D analytical geometry configuration, gravity and lateral load allocations, preliminary member sizing, and finite element meshing.

Deliverables Analytical Models (.edb, .pro, .sdb), Force Diagram Packages, Deflection Check Sheets.
02 / CLASH COORDINATION
Structural BIM Coordination (LOD 200–350)

Parametric concrete and steel modeling with multi-disciplinary clash detection across MEP and architectural systems prior to construction.

Deliverables Revit Models (.rvt), Navisworks Federated Coordination Reports (.nwc/.nwd), IFC Exchanges.
03 / REINFORCED CONCRETE
Reinforced Concrete Detailing

3D placement modeling of rebar cages, slab layout detailing, bar shape coding, and automated schedule generation per standard detailing manuals.

Deliverables General Arrangement (GA) Drawings, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), Rebar Callouts.
04 / STRUCTURAL STEEL
Structural Steel Shop Drafting

Fabrication-level structural steel framing, connection modeling, bolt and weld scheduling, and plate preparation layouts.

Deliverables Assembly Drawings, Erection Layout Plans, CNC Data Outputs, Steel Material Takeoffs.
01 / SCHEMATIC GRIDS
Structural Grid & Column Layout Planning

Coordination with architectural floor plans to align column grids, optimize spans, minimize beam drops, and eliminate transfer conflicts.

Deliverables Schematic Structural Grid Layouts, Column Placement Options, Vertical Clearance Studies.
02 / WIND & SEISMIC
Lateral Stability & Shear Core Planning

Positioning of shear walls, reinforced elevator/stair cores, and braced frames for optimal torsional resistance and stiffness symmetry.

Deliverables Center of Mass vs. Rigidity Diagrams, Shear Core Framing Schematics, Drift Allocation Plans.
03 / TYPOLOGY STUDIES
Framing System Evaluation & Selection

Comparative evaluation of solid slabs, flat slabs with drop panels, post-tensioned systems, ribbed slabs, or steel decking based on spans and cost.

Deliverables Structural Scheme Option Reports, Quantity/Cost Comparison Matrix, Deflection Sensitivity Studies.
04 / GEOTECHNICAL INTERFACE
Substructure & Foundation Strategy

Preliminary foundation layout formulation based on geotechnical parameters, assessing isolated footings, strips, raft/mat, or piled solutions.

Deliverables Preliminary Substructure Layouts, Foundation Load Takeoff Schedules, Earthwork Interface Models.
Software Matrix

Computational & BIM Toolset

Autodesk Robot Structural Analysis

Bi-directional Revit link for 3D frame/shell FEA, wind/seismic load simulations, and Eurocode/BS concrete and steel member checks.

Compliance & Standards

Supported Design Codes

Eurocode 0 (EN 1990) & Eurocode 1 (EN 1991)

Basis of structural design and gravity, imposed, wind, and seismic load actions.

Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-1) & BS 8110

Reinforced concrete design executed alongside IStructE Standard Detailing Manuals and BS 8666 Bar Shape Codes.

Eurocode 3 (EN 1993-1-1 / 1-8) & BS 5950

Structural steelwork member design, bolted/welded connection checks, and shop fabrication tolerances.

EN 1992-4 & ETAG 001 / EAD Guidelines

Post-installed rebar and baseplate anchor verifications backed by auditable fischer FiXperience calculation reports.

Kenya Building Code & NCA Standards

Local regulatory alignment formatted for National Construction Authority (NCA) and county building permit submissions.

Quality Assurance

5-Stage Project Delivery Methodology

Gated quality milestones from initial architectural grid audit to final Professional Engineer audit dossier handover.

STAGE 01
Ingestion & Grid Audit

Review architectural drawings, MEP spatial requirements, and geotechnical parameters; establish standard building grid, levels, and origins.

Grid Alignment Sheet & Discrepancy Log
STAGE 02
Analytical & BIM Modeling

Build parametric 3D models in Revit, push wireframes bi-directionally to Robot FEA for load envelopes, and verify baseplate/rebar anchors.

Sizing Package & Node Integrity Check
STAGE 03
Clash Detection & Coordination

Federate structural models against architectural and MEP services in Navisworks to resolve penetrations, sleeves, and headroom limits.

Interference Clearance Report
STAGE 04
Detailing & Fabrication

Generate reinforcement layouts, hooks/laps, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), steel connection assemblies, and complete drafting sheets.

Full GA Drawing Set & BBS
STAGE 05
Engineering Handover & Audit

Compile native analytical files (.rtd, .edb, .pro, .sdb), model exports, and drawing sets for review and stamping by the supervising PE.

Final Engineer Audit Dossier
