Who We Are

Operating at the Intersection of Computational Engineering & Digital Construction.

Astra Structural BIM is the specialized structural engineering modeling, building planning, and Building Information Modeling (BIM) consultancy division of Astrasoft. We serve consulting structural engineers, architects, main contractors, and real estate developers with precision-engineered 3D analytical models and fabrication-ready detailing packages.

Technical Capabilities

End-to-End Structural Planning & BIM Modeling

.EDB / .PRO / .SDB

Computational Structural Modeling

Full 3D analytical geometry configuration, gravity and lateral load allocations, preliminary member sizing, and finite element meshing.

Deliverables Analytical models (.edb, .pro, .sdb), force diagram packages, and deflection check sheets.
LOD 200 to 350

Structural BIM Coordination

Parametric structural concrete and steel modeling with multi-disciplinary clash detection across MEP and architectural systems prior to construction.

Deliverables Revit models (.rvt), Navisworks federated coordination reports (.nwc/.nwd), and IFC exchanges.
GA & BBS PACKAGES

Reinforced Concrete Detailing

3D placement modeling of rebar cages, slab layout detailing, bar shape coding, and automated bar schedule generation per standard detailing manuals.

Deliverables Comprehensive General Arrangement (GA) drawings, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), and rebar callouts.
CNC & SHOP DRAFTING

Structural Steel Shop Drafting

Fabrication-level structural steel framing, connection modeling, bolt and weld scheduling, and plate preparation layouts.

Deliverables Assembly drawings, erection layout plans, CNC data outputs, and steel material takeoffs.
SCHEMATIC GRIDS

Structural Grid & Column Layout Planning

Coordination with architectural floor plans to align column grids, optimize spans, minimize beam drops in usable spaces, and eliminate transfer conflicts.

Deliverables Schematic Structural Grid Layouts, Column Placement Options, and Vertical Clearance Studies.
WIND & SEISMIC

Lateral Stability & Shear Core Planning

Positioning of shear walls, reinforced elevator/stair cores, and braced frames to ensure optimal torsional resistance, stiffness symmetry, and wind/seismic stability.

Deliverables Center of Mass vs. Rigidity Diagrams, Shear Core Framing Schematics, and Drift Allocation Plans.
TYPOLOGY MATRIX

Framing System Evaluation & Selection

Comparative evaluation of solid slabs with beams, flat slabs with drop panels, post-tensioned systems, ribbed slabs, or steel decking based on spans and cost.

Deliverables Structural Scheme Option Reports, Quantity/Cost Comparison Matrix, and Deflection Sensitivity Studies.
SUBSTRUCTURE

Substructure & Foundation Strategy

Preliminary foundation layout formulation based on geotechnical data, assessing isolated footings, strip footings, raft/mat foundations, or piled solutions.

Deliverables Preliminary Substructure Layouts, Foundation Load Takeoff Schedules, and Earthwork Interface Models.

BIM & FEA STACK

Engineering Software Matrix

RVT
Autodesk Revit

Builds 3D physical and analytical models (LOD 200 to 350), models 3D rebar, generates automated Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), and exports .rvt / .ifc files.

RSA
Autodesk Robot Structural Analysis

Connects bi-directionally with Revit to run 3D frame/shell analysis, wind/seismic load simulations, and Eurocode/BS concrete and steel checks.

NWD
Autodesk Navisworks Manage

Federates structural, architectural, and MEP models for automated clash detection, identifying penetrations, sleeve requirements, and headroom conflicts.

DWG
AutoCAD

Handles fast 2D General Arrangement (GA) drafting, standard county/municipal submission sheet cleanups, and legacy .dwg exchange.

FIX
fischer FiXperience

Verifies steel-to-concrete baseplates, post-installed rebar connections (REBAR-FIX), masonry anchors (C-FIX), and CFRP strengthening (REINFORCE-FIX).

CODES & STATUTORY

Supported Design Codes & Detailing Standards

EC0/1
Basis of Structural Design & Actions

Eurocode 0 (EN 1990) and Eurocode 1 (EN 1991: Gravity, Wind & Seismic Actions).

EC2
Reinforced Concrete Design & Detailing

Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-1), BS 8110, and Standard Detailing Manuals (IStructE / BS 8666 Bar Shape Codes).

EC3
Structural Steelwork & Connections

Eurocode 3 (EN 1993-1-1/1-8) and BS 5950 for member stability and connection tolerances.

ETAG
Post-Installed Rebar & Baseplate Anchors

EN 1992-4 and ETAG 001 / EAD Guidelines verified with auditable fischer FiXperience printouts.

NCA
Local Regulatory Alignment

Kenya Building Code and National Construction Authority (NCA) submission standards.

Quality Assurance

5-Stage Project Delivery Methodology

Select any stage below to inspect its quality verification gate and handover deliverables.

STAGE 01
Ingestion & Grid Audit

Review architectural drawings, MEP spatial requirements, and geotechnical parameters. Establish standard building grid, structural levels, and coordinate origins.

Grid Alignment Sheet & Discrepancy Log
STAGE 02
Analytical & BIM Modeling

Build parametric 3D models in Revit and push wireframes bi-directionally into Robot FEA. Apply gravity/wind/seismic envelopes and verify anchors in fischer FiXperience.

Preliminary Sizing & Node Integrity Check
STAGE 03
Clash Detection & Coordination

Federate structural models against architectural and MEP services in Navisworks to resolve structural penetrations, sleeve requirements, and headroom constraints.

Interference Clearance Report
STAGE 04
Detailing & Fabrication Packages

Generate reinforcement layouts, standard hooks/laps, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), steel connection assemblies, and complete drafting sheets.

Full General Arrangement (GA) Set & BBS
STAGE 05
Engineering Handover & Audit

Package native analytical calculation files, model exports, and drawing sets for review and formal stamping by the supervising Professional Engineer (PE).

Final Engineer Audit Dossier & Permit Set
