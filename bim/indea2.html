Structural & Building Planning Services

Structural Grid & Column Layout Planning

Coordination with architectural floor plans to align column grids, optimize spans, minimize beam drops in usable spaces, and eliminate structural transfer conflicts.

Deliverables Schematic Structural Grid Layouts, Column Placement Options, Vertical Clearance Studies.
Lateral Stability & Shear Core Planning

Positioning of shear walls, reinforced elevator/stair cores, and braced frames to ensure optimal torsional resistance, stiffness symmetry, and wind/seismic stability.

Deliverables Center of Mass vs. Rigidity Diagrams, Shear Core Framing Schematics, Drift Allocation Plans.
Framing System Evaluation & Selection

Comparative evaluation of framing typologies—solid slab with beams, flat slab with drop panels, post-tensioned systems, ribbed slabs, or structural steel decking—based on spans and cost.

Deliverables Structural Scheme Option Reports, Quantity/Cost Comparison Matrix, Deflection Sensitivity Studies.
Substructure & Foundation Strategy

Preliminary foundation layout formulation based on geotechnical data, assessing isolated footings, strip footings, raft/mat foundations, or piled solutions.

Deliverables Preliminary Substructure Layouts, Foundation Load Takeoff Schedules, Earthwork Interface Models.

Core Technical Modeling & Analysis Services

Computational Structural Modeling

Full 3D analytical geometry configuration, gravity and lateral load allocations, preliminary member sizing, and finite element meshing.

Deliverables Analytical Models (.edb, .pro, .sdb), Force Diagram Packages, Deflection Check Sheets.
Structural BIM Coordination

Parametric structural concrete and steel modeling with multi-disciplinary clash detection across MEP and architectural systems (LOD 200–350).

Deliverables Revit Models (.rvt), Navisworks Federated Coordination Reports (.nwc/.nwd), IFC Exchanges.
Reinforced Concrete Detailing

3D placement modeling of rebar cages, slab layout detailing, bar shape coding, and automated schedule generation per standard detailing manuals.

Deliverables Comprehensive General Arrangement (GA) Drawings, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), Rebar Callouts.
Structural Steel Shop Drafting

Fabrication-level structural steel framing, connection modeling, bolt and weld scheduling, and plate preparation layouts.

Deliverables Assembly Drawings, Erection Layout Plans, CNC Data Outputs, Steel Material Takeoffs.

Engineering Software Stack

Autodesk Revit

Builds 3D physical and analytical structural models (LOD 200–350), models 3D rebar, generates automated Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), and exports .rvt / .ifc files.

Autodesk Robot Structural Analysis

Connects bi-directionally with Revit to execute 3D frame/shell FEA, wind and seismic load simulations, and Eurocode/BS concrete and steel checks.

Autodesk Navisworks Manage

Federates structural, architectural, and MEP models for automated clash detection, identifying penetrations, sleeve requirements, and headroom conflicts (.nwc/.nwd).

AutoCAD

Executes fast 2D General Arrangement (GA) drafting, county/municipal submission sheet cleanups, and legacy .dwg file exchange.

fischer FiXperience

Verifies steel-to-concrete baseplates, post-installed rebar connections (REBAR-FIX), masonry anchors (C-FIX), and CFRP strengthening (REINFORCE-FIX).

Quality Assurance

5-Stage Project Delivery Methodology

Every project passes through five gated verification milestones to guarantee precision, constructability, and rapid Professional Engineer sign-off.

Stage 01
Ingestion & Grid Audit

Review architectural drawings, MEP spatial requirements, and geotechnical parameters. Establish standard building grid, structural levels, and coordinate origins.

Grid Alignment Verification Sheet & Discrepancy Log
Stage 02
Analytical & BIM Modeling

Build parametric 3D models in Revit and push analytical wireframes bi-directionally into Robot FEA. Apply gravity/wind/seismic loads and verify anchors in fischer FiXperience.

Preliminary Sizing & Analytical Node Integrity Check
Stage 03
Clash Detection & Coordination

Federate structural models against architectural and MEP services in Navisworks to resolve structural penetrations, sleeve requirements, and headroom constraints.

Interference Clearance Report & Penetration Drawings
Stage 04
Detailing & Fabrication Packages

Generate reinforcement layouts, standard hooks/laps, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), steel connection assemblies, and complete drafting sheets.

Full General Arrangement (GA) Set & BBS
Stage 05
Engineering Handover & Audit

Package native analytical calculation files, model exports, and drawing sets for review and formal stamping by the supervising Professional Engineer (PE).

Final Engineer Audit Dossier & Permit Ready Set
