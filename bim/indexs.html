Mission & Vision

Setting the Benchmark for Constructible Building Delivery.

Our Mission

To be Africa's premier digital structural modeling and computational engineering partner, setting the benchmark for clash-free, data-driven, and constructible building delivery.

Our Vision

To empower engineering consultancies, architects, and builders with high-precision 3D structural modeling, finite element analysis, and automated detailing workflows that eliminate on-site errors, accelerate project approvals, and optimize material efficiency.[cite: 3]

Capabilities

Structural Planning & Core Technical Modeling

Structural & Building Planning

Grid & Column Layout

Architectural coordination to optimize spans, minimize beam drops, and eliminate transfer conflicts.

Lateral Stability

Positioning shear walls and cores for optimal torsional resistance, stiffness, and wind/seismic stability.

Framing Selection

Comparative evaluation of solid, flat, ribbed, or PT slabs based on spans and cost.

Substructure Strategy

Preliminary foundation layouts assessing isolated footings, mats, or piled solutions.[cite: 6]

Core Technical Modeling

Computational Structural Modeling

Full 3D analytical geometry configuration, load allocations, and finite element meshing. Delivering analytical models, force diagrams, and deflection checks.

Structural BIM Coordination

Parametric concrete and steel modeling with multi-disciplinary clash detection against MEP and architectural systems (LOD 200-350).

Reinforced Concrete Detailing

Structural Steel Shop Drafting

Delivery Methodology

5-Stage Quality Assurance Workflow

Stage 1
Ingestion & Grid Audit

Review architectural/MEP data, establish standard building grids, levels, and coordinate origins.

Grid Alignment Verification Log

Stage 2
Analytical & BIM Modeling

Build 3D structural models in Revit, execute Robot FEA analysis, and verify anchors in fischer FiXperience.

Preliminary Sizing & Anchorage Package

Stage 3
Clash Detection

Federate models in Navisworks to identify structural penetrations and sleeve/headroom requirements.

Interference Clearance Report

Stage 4
Detailing & Fabrication

Generate reinforcement layouts, standard hooks/laps, BBS, and steel connection assemblies.

Full GA Drawing Set & BBS

Stage 5
Engineering Handover

Package analytical files and drawing sets for review and stamping by the supervising Professional Engineer (PE).

Final Engineer Audit Dossier[cite: 10, 11]
